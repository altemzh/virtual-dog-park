Dog Encyclopedia

Beagle

  • Size: Small to medium, about 9–11 kg.
  • Personality: Curious, cheerful and led by its nose.
  • Care: Needs daily walks and sniffing games. Keep food out of reach!

Golden Retriever

  • Size: Large, about 25–34 kg.
  • Personality: Gentle, loyal and eager to please.
  • Care: Loves swimming and fetch. Brush its thick coat a few times a week.

Poodle

  • Size: Comes in toy, miniature and standard sizes.
  • Personality: Smart, playful and quick to learn tricks.
  • Care: Curly coat needs regular brushing and trims. Enjoys puzzle toys.
